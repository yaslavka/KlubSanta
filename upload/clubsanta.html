Questions Families Ask Before Entering the Circle

Thoughtful answers for parents, grandparents, and keepers of the tradition.

What makes Klub Santa different from a standard Santa call?

Most seasonal calls are one-off novelties. Klub Santa is built as a multi-year family ritual and community. We remember your family's story from year to year, speak in 8 languages, pair calls with tangible wax-sealed keepsakes, and dedicate 10% of our work to orphans and isolated elders.

How does Santa know my child's or loved one's personal details?

When you reserve your ritual, you share a few quiet notes in our ledger - their name, pronunciation, age, proudest moments from the year, and family traditions. Santa weaves those exact memories naturally into the conversation.

Is Klub Santa only for young children?

Not at all. While children are at the heart of the ritual, many members gift calls and letters to older kids who are beginning to doubt, to spouses, or to grandparents and elders who cherish a warm voice on Christmas Eve.

Which languages can Santa speak with our family?

Santa currently speaks with families in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Mandarin, and Cantonese, honoring each household's cultural heritage.

Can we keep a recording of the call for future Christmases?

Yes. Families who choose The Complete Keepsake Ritual or Klub Santa Circle Membership receive a private keepsake archive so you can replay the moment years later.

How does the 10% charity mission work?

Ten percent of every seasonal ritual is dedicated to bringing Christmas gifts, letters, and warm calls to children in orphanages and isolated seniors in care homes.
